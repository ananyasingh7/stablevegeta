# The Vision

Most AI assistants today are:
- Cloud-based and privacy-invasive
- Generic and not personalized to individual needs
- Disconnected from personal data sources

## What if we flipped this model?

Imagine an AI assistant that:

1. **Runs locally** on your own hardware (Mac Studio, for example)
2. **Integrates with your data** - health metrics, calendars, notes, projects
3. **Understands your context** - your goals, preferences, routines
4. **Respects your privacy** - data never leaves your machine

## Use Cases

- Health coaching based on WHOOP data
- Project management and task prioritization
- Research assistant for your specific interests
- Morning briefings with personalized insights

## Challenges

- Local compute requirements (need good hardware)
- Data integration complexity
- Model selection and fine-tuning
- UI/UX for daily interaction

## Next Steps

Start small:
- Pick one data source (e.g., WHOOP health data)
- Build one workflow (e.g., daily health check-in)
- Iterate based on real usage
